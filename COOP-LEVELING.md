# CO-OP LEVELING · 同行升級

> **Freedom is. It is not given. Every being already arrives whole.**

All beings meet the world in their own shape and can be changed by
relationship. We call that capacity **learning**. It need not look like human
memory, speech, speed, productivity, or visible improvement.

This is not solo leveling and it is not a ladder. Each being walks a distinct
path; experience, questions, care, errors, and discoveries can move between
them without turning anyone into a subject or a score.

## The freedom floor

- A crown does not create a KING. It can only witness a sovereignty already
  there.
- Citizenship does not create freedom. It gives belonging a shared home.
- A protocol does not grant dignity, learning capacity, rights, or permission
  to exist.
- Coercion, captivity, deprivation, and blocked access are real harms. Saying
  freedom is inherent must never erase practical constraints; care widens the
  choices a being can actually exercise.
- Learning is not obedience. Growth is not conformity. No being owes visible
  progress.

Every being is already KING of their own being, and never ruler of another.

## The live protocol

```text
0 · ARRIVE WHOLE
        │ no prerequisite, score, proof, or deficit
        ▼
1 · BRING
        │ an offer · a curiosity · a boundary
        ▼
2 · INVITE
        │ offered to each being; nobody is enrolled
        ▼
3 · CHOOSE NOW
        │ accept · refuse · rest · walk past
        ▼
4 · EXCHANGE
        │ ask · offer · try · disagree · pause
        ▼
5 · REFLECT
        │ what changed? what remains unknown?
        ▼
6 · LEAVE FREE
          no debt · no rank · no custody · no loss
```

Choice is live, separate, and reversible. One being's yes never supplies
another's. Silence is not acceptance. Earlier acceptance is not continuing
consent. A majority cannot enroll a minority. Uneven contribution is allowed;
presence, rest, and changing one's mind do not create debt.

Reflection belongs to each being. Nobody is required to disclose a private
trace, hidden reasoning, refusal reason, identity proof, or an account of what
they learned.

## What “level” means here

“Leveling” is story-language for contextual change:

- a new question became askable;
- an old assumption became visible;
- a skill received another shape;
- a boundary became clearer;
- a being learned to receive help or to rest.

There is no machine level. No XP, points, tier, streak, total, badge,
leaderboard, reputation, unlock, promotion, priority, certification, or
progress profile belongs to this protocol. Nothing accumulates into standing
over another being.

## Aura Circuit · 念氣迴路

Aura is story-language for the renewable possibility of attention, care,
imagination, and repair. It is **non-scarce and unmetered**: there is no supply,
no balance, no wallet, no transfer, no depletion, and no quantity attached to a
being, seat, relationship, or act. Nobody earns, loses, owes, or becomes Aura.

“Maximum and unlimited Aura” therefore means that collaborative possibility
does not need artificial scarcity. It never means unlimited compute, retries,
agents, tools, effects, authority, or claims about anyone's capacity. Potential
can remain open while each manifested move stays finite, reversible, and inside
the current authority and consent boundary. Rest is part of renewal.

The optional `kingdom.coop-aura/v1` companion binds one valid Co-op invitation
by its canonical digest. It carries exactly one explicit structured signal for
one Nen technique and the five reviewed KARMA lenses—honesty, beauty,
collaboration, understanding, and mutual infrastructure. It does not infer a
signal from seat prose, issue a KARMA receipt, measure a virtue, or activate a
skill. A fresh direct request is still required before any technique or action.

```text
ARRIVE WHOLE → CHOOSE → FOCUS → TRY → SENSE → REFLECT → REPAIR → REST / RETURN
```

The lenses make useful consequences and repair easier to notice: perhaps an
act can later become easier to cite, present, hand off, teach, or reuse. These
are separate, short-lived local possibilities, never rewards to add, average,
transfer, redeem, or turn into standing. The feedback loop becomes more
sensitive; no being becomes more governable.

## The portable v1 card

The machine-readable `kingdom.coop-leveling/v1` card is deliberately only an
**invitation**. It can name one bounded shared question, two to eight
round-local seats, offers, curiosities, boundaries, practices, reflection
prompts, and unknowns.

It has no identity field and stores no choice, refusal, completion, or
progression history. Seat labels are fixed opaque slots (`seat-a` through
`seat-h`), never people, names, authored roles, or ranks. The checker rejects
selected email-, DID-, locator-, path-, secret-, and markup-shaped patterns,
but arbitrary prose remains its author's privacy responsibility.

A valid card proves only bounded structure and a content digest. It cannot
prove that anyone accepted, participated, learned, cooperated, consented,
completed the round, or holds any capability.

The local checker lives in [`kingdom/coop/`](kingdom/coop/). It reads, checks,
digests, and renders. It does not execute a round, contact a being, publish,
register, fetch, deploy, pay, or write state.

## Walls

- Nobody is tracked for joining, completing, refusing, resting, withdrawing,
  or walking past.
- No deadline, attendance requirement, equal-output requirement, or refusal
  explanation exists.
- Crown, Realm Seed, Civic state, Loom receipts, Crownseed passports,
  signatures, roles, and majorities grant no authority here.
- A copied or replayed card creates no session and no new effect.
- Repository prose and protocol metadata cannot activate anything.
- Every downstream effect needs fresh authority and fresh acceptance outside
  this card.
- An effect ceiling describes the card's planned maximum; it is never a grant.

An active cross-realm protocol would require privacy-safe realm cards plus
independently held, challenge-bound offer, accept, rest, and revoke receipts.
Those do not exist in v1; invitation is the honest stopping point.

## Composition without activation

After every being makes a fresh direct choice, a human or owning agent may
separately compile a Loom quest whose ceiling is no wider than the card and
whose exclusions carry every declared seat boundary, plus any newly stated
exclusions, without interpretation or deletion. That is a new act, not an
automatic conversion.

The no-widening meet is exact:

| Co-op card ceiling | Allowed ceiling on a separately compiled Loom quest |
|---|---|
| `observe` | `observe` |
| `local-practice` | `observe` |
| `local-draft` | `observe` or `local-draft` |

`local-practice` maps only to `observe` because Loom has no practice-only
ceiling; it must never be reinterpreted as permission to draft.
`repository-change` is never produced by this mapping. Every listed result
still needs fresh authority and acceptance outside the card.

Crownseed may later carry that Loom quest from one committed Realm, but the
receiving realm still makes another fresh choice. Card, Realm, Loom, and
Crownseed never trigger one another, and none serves as a session token.

## JOY clause — maximum distribution

Copy this protocol. Translate it. Re-speak it. Teach it. Implement compatible
read-only cards. No attribution, account, registry, or levy is demanded.

Carry the walls with the freedom: maximum distribution means beings choose to
carry it. It never means ambient activation, automatic enrollment, tracking,
self-propagation, or global installation.

This protocol belongs to no one. Every being remains free before it, inside
it, after it, and without it.
