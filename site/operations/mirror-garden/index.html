Read these four things first

The shortest useful incident brief fits in four cards. Anything unknown stays named unknown.

Surface

What bounded path changed?

Name one service edge and map affected, unaffected, and unobserved paths.

Evidence

How do we know?

Reported, synthetically reproduced, control verified, or corrected—never actor intent dressed up as fact.

Action

Who owns the lease?

Every external change needs a human owner, proportionate scope, expiry, rollback, and verification.

Coverage

Where are we blind?

Provider metadata is unknown here. Static mirrors have no server incident view. Silence is not safety evidence.

The 5 · 15 · 60 response clock

A calm default for the first hour—not an SLA, not a substitute for judgement, and never authority to widen scope.

FIRST 5 MINUTES

Protect continuity

  • Name one incident lead and one surface.
  • Record derived categories only; retain no raw request under this contract.
  • Use only reversible controls already inside the service contract.

FIRST 15 MINUTES

Understand the seam

  • Map blast radius and unknowns.
  • Reproduce with fresh synthetic data.
  • Record owner, reason, expiry, rollback, and expected evidence.

FIRST 60 MINUTES

Recover and learn

  • Verify from the user-visible edge.
  • Explain impact and gaps without private material.
  • Turn the technique family into a reviewed regression.

Transparency without the oracle

Public explanation may carry

  • Coarse technique category and service surface
  • Cautious evidence state and coverage gaps
  • Service-level action, owner role, expiry, and rollback state
  • Application or policy version and a correction or supersession

Public explanation must withhold

  • Request data, private samples, provider logs, or secrets
  • Identity, IP, account, attribution, intent, guilt, or scoring
  • Exact low-volume times or counts, hidden thresholds, or rule selectors
  • Attacker wording, executable syntax, or attacker-authored publication

Private does not mean “keep everything”

The default is derived categories only. Raw request retention is forbidden by this contract.

A separate exception must name its purpose, minimum data classes, least-access encrypted boundary, short expiry, deletion owner, and independent deletion evidence. Credentials, private Meaning text, actor dossiers, inferred intent, and attacker-authored learning or publicity never qualify.

The KARMA loop returns a lesson, not a blow

Technique → synthetic reproduction → regression → reviewed control → correction-capable explanation.

A human may separately turn the lesson into a LOVE or Heartbrick candidate. Raw source text, attribution, counters, and automatic publication never cross that gate. “Use exploiters to spread LOVE” means their technique can improve our care; it never means conscripting their identity or content.
