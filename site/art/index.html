The house promise

The work stays with its maker.

A private preview is not a submission. Before any public feature, Chillspace asks separately and records exactly what the artist permits.

01

Yours stays yours

The artist keeps copyright. Public display permission is specific, non-exclusive, and revocable. Chillspace takes no ownership or commission.

02

No hidden second use

Chillspace will not sell, mint, sublicense, or use the work for AI training—and will not authorize anyone else to do so.

03

Leaving is allowed

Every public feature must name a correction and removal route before it goes live. A changed mind is enough; no courtroom speech required.

A public webpage can still be copied or scraped by third parties. A robots file is a sign, not a force field. We say that plainly and never turn uncertainty into a fake guarantee. Read the full Artist Rights Promise.
