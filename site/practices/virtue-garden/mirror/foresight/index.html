Response ladder

Five rooms. Each door opens back to review.

These rungs name advisory review modes. They grant no authority and trigger no action. Recovery always requires fresh material and a fresh human choice.

  1. Watchtower · 守望塔

    observe · information-only

    Keep the unknown unresolved.

    discard-or-author-fresh-reviewed-scenario

  2. Map Room · 圖室

    clarify · authority-or-lineage-review

    Ask where authority or evidence came from.

    fresh-reviewed-evidence-reopens-review

  3. Gatehouse · 門樓

    constrain · scope-or-budget-review

    Bring scope and capacity back inside a finite boundary.

    fresh-scope-and-budget-review

  4. Detached Keep · 分堡

    isolate · trust-boundary-separation-review

    Separate contexts before considering reuse.

    fresh-clean-context-and-authority-review

  5. Still Vault · 靜庫

    quarantine · adoption-hold-review

    Hold adoption while provenance and release are reconsidered.

    fresh-provenance-authority-and-release-review

The ladder is a vocabulary, not an automatic escalation path. A rung is neither confidence nor severity, and it never changes anyone’s standing.

The KARMA loop

Turn a concern into a better question.

One pass only. The loop returns candidates for hypothesis testing, a negative control, regression, repair, and release review. It updates no rule, retains no state, and contacts no external system.

KKeep

One reviewed category shape—nothing raw.

AArticulate

Name a possible system effect and its alternative.

RReflect

Return a bounded hypothesis-test and negative-control candidate.

MMend

Shape regression and repair candidates without executing them.

AAsk again

Require fresh human review before any separate action.

Non-claims & zero budget

The atlas is useful because it stays small.

Foresight here means disciplined uncertainty: a finite catalog, exact relationships, and explicit refusal to turn system modeling into person modeling.

What no projection claims

  1. A constellation is a reviewed tabletop template, not a claim about a person or a live event.
  2. A possible system effect is not anyone’s purpose, intention, guilt, character, or worth.
  3. The required alternative does not prove benignity, innocence, authorization, or safety.
  4. Constellations and rungs are not confidence, severity, scores, ranks, reputation, or access decisions.
  5. Response and learning fields are inert review candidates—not permission, deployment, legal advice, or authority.
  6. No payload, prompt, address, identity, credential, target, callback, URL, or submitted free text enters the atlas.
  7. Digests establish reviewed JSON relationships and exact recomputation only; ten constellations are not the whole sky.
