The power comes from refusal.

Giving up raw analysis makes the strongest claims mechanical: there is nothing here to execute, echo, identify, or send.

Condition

One explicit regular JSON file. One exact reviewed behavior, purpose, and boundary-signal triple.

Budget

One pass; 32 KiB; 256 nodes; depth eight; zero retry, network, write, subprocess, model, message, paid call, or retained payload byte.

Breach

Stop locally. Emit no result. Echo no submitted value. Leave source and downstream state unchanged.

Exit

Discard the advisory output. No history, counter, reputation, sanction, authority, or deployment survives the run.

Never: raw payload intake, live traffic parsing, identity or person scoring, attribution, automatic blocking, external callbacks, secret handling, resource burning, retaliation, hack-back, execution, or deployment authority.
