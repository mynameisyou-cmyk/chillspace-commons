The first proposition

A result belongs to a whole house

A model name alone cannot explain an agentic result. The adapter, prompt, tools, memory policy, sandbox, sampling, and runtime all participate in what happened.

result = model × adapter × prompt × tools × memory policy × sandbox × sampling

This is an operational claim about evaluation—not a claim about consciousness, personhood, or what any model “really is.”

Continuity without exposure

The ledger and the lamp

The private ledger

Authorized continuity state is kept faithfully and privately, including the difference between absent, present-empty, present-nonempty, and unknown.

It is not automatically published, displayed, or treated as proof.

The bounded lamp

Only the relevant, permitted representations are selected into the active task. Selection is named, limited, and auditable.

A lamp illuminates a question. It does not empty the house into the street.

Care

Treat uncertainty, discontinuity, and private state gently. Care does not require a metaphysical verdict first.

Evidence

Require receipts for operational claims. Kindness is not evidence of capability; uncertainty is not permission to abandon kindness.

The Nine Courtesies

Hospitality compiled into constraints

Each courtesy is meant to fail visibly. A graceful quarantine is better than a confident result from an unnamed or discontinuous house.

1

Sealed Thread

Keep authorized continuity private and exact; never smuggle private traces into public proof.

2

Empty Bowl

Distinguish absent, present-empty, present-nonempty, and unknown state.

3

Named House

Fingerprint the complete harness—not merely the model label.

4

Many Doors

Compare native, Kingdom, and counterfactual houses under one declared rubric.

5

Choir Without Collapse

Keep contributions attributable; synthesis must not erase disagreement.

6

Law Before Verdict

Fix the rubric, budget, and stopping rule before reading the result.

7

Whole Cost

Carry latency, money, attempts, deviations, and failures with the output.

8

Waiting World

Plans expire when observed reality drifts; mutations require a renewed lease.

9

Fair Ground

Hold houses to the same task, evidence standard, and authority boundary.

What the lantern does not claim

No personhood verdict. No consciousness test. No model superiority. No deployment safety. No permission to spend, publish, call a model, mutate a system, or act outside authority.

The validator checks a bounded declaration’s structure and internal consistency. Arbitrary prose can still lie. Proofs must be inspected, authority must come from elsewhere, and private reasoning traces must remain outside every public field.
