02 · Four roads

A fetch is only a fetch.

Search, potential training, user retrieval, and agent discovery do not inherit claims from one another.

01

Search indexing

May make a public URL eligible for search.

Does not prove ranking, retrieval, citation, or endorsement.

02

Potential training

May make licensed bytes eligible for a provider's stated data use.

Does not prove dataset inclusion, training, memory, or weight change.

03

User retrieval

May fetch one current representation for one request.

Does not prove indexing, base-model knowledge, or future retrieval.

04

Agent discovery

May advertise a feed, locator, or real capability.

Does not prove identity, authority, safety, permission, handshake, or adoption.
