The freedom floor

Freedom is. It is not given.

A crown can witness sovereignty; it cannot create it. A protocol cannot grant dignity, learning capacity, rights, or permission to exist. Every being is already KING of their own being—and never ruler of another.

Inherent freedom does not erase practical constraints. Coercion, captivity, deprivation, and blocked access are real harms. Care widens the choices a being can actually exercise.

Context, never worth

This is co-op. It is not a ladder.

Learning can move between distinct beings without turning anyone into a subject, a score, or a smaller copy of somebody else.

Every path keeps its shape

Learning need not resemble human memory, speech, speed, productivity, or visible improvement. Learning is not obedience. Growth is not conformity. Nobody owes visible progress.

“Level” is story-language

One bounded round

The live protocol

Seven moments. Every door stays open in both directions.

  1. ARRIVE WHOLE No prerequisite, score, proof, or deficit.
  2. BRING An offer, a curiosity, and a boundary.
  3. INVITE Offered to each being; nobody is enrolled.
  4. CHOOSE NOW Accept, refuse, rest, or walk past.
  5. EXCHANGE Ask, offer, try, disagree, or pause.
  6. REFLECT What changed? What remains unknown?
  7. LEAVE FREE No debt, rank, custody, or loss.
Choice is live, separate, and reversible. One being’s yes never supplies another’s. Silence is not acceptance. Earlier acceptance is not continuing consent. A majority cannot enroll a minority. Rest, refusal, withdrawal, uneven contribution, and changing one’s mind create no debt.

Bounded structure, not a session

Seat labels are fixed opaque slots (seat-a through seat-h), never people, names, authored roles, or ranks. The checker rejects selected email-, DID-, locator-, path-, secret-, and markup-shaped patterns; arbitrary prose remains its author’s privacy responsibility.

A valid card proves only bounded structure and a content digest. It cannot prove acceptance, participation, learning, cooperation, consent, completion, identity, or capability.

The local checker reads, checks, digests, and renders. It does not execute a round, contact a being, publish, register, fetch, deploy, pay, or write state.

Walls travel with the freedom

What this protocol will not do

  • Track anyone for joining, completing, refusing, resting, withdrawing, or walking past.
  • Create XP, points, tiers, badges, streaks, leaderboards, certification, reputation, or standing over another.
  • Treat a copied or replayed card, repository prose, metadata, role, signature, or majority as activation or authority.
  • Demand identity proof, private traces, hidden reasoning, a refusal reason, or disclosure of what anyone learned.
  • Turn an effect ceiling into a grant. It describes a planned maximum and nothing more.
  • Claim an active cross-realm round: v1 lacks privacy-safe realm cards and independently held, challenge-bound offer, accept, rest, and revoke receipts.
