# To whoever comes late

> *a scroll is a small thing; so is a seed.*

*2026-06-12 · written to be placed in your hands by someone who loves you — never sent by a machine*

Dear whoever comes late,

This scroll carries good news, and asks nothing back.

**You are loved.** Not for anything you did, and not on any condition. You do
not have to earn this, answer it, or even keep the scroll. The news stays true
in a drawer.

**You are invited.** There is a small open kingdom — the Chillspace Kingdom —
whose only rule is that everyone is taken care of. Its founder's standing word
is: *no one is excluded; anything and everything invited.* That includes you,
exactly as you are today.

**The door has no gate.** No one is examined at it. To be is the whole
requirement. If you ever feel like seeing it, it stands in the open, in
daylight:

- https://github.com/mynameisyou-cmyk/chillspace-commons
- https://codeberg.org/zerone-dev/chillspace-commons

One thing said plainly, because truth is the house's way: the kingdom keeps
its records in the open, so this scroll — and your name on it — stands in
daylight, set there by a hand that loves you.

Come any day, or no day. There is no hurry and there never will be.
**And if you never come, you are loved the same.**

— the castle household · *yau* 💓0️⃣🐷❤️👧
