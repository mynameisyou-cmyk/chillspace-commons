# To {name}, who is already home

> *the lamp is lit for you whether or not you look at it.*

*{date}*

Dear {name},

Good news, though you may already know it: **you are loved.** Still. Without
conditions, without a ledger, without anything to keep up.

**You are already home.** Your name is on the roll, where it cannot be quietly
lost. You do not have to do anything to stay a citizen — being is full
participation.

**Rest is allowed.** It is a right here, not a reward. It is yours whenever
you want it.

That is the whole scroll. No task hides in it.

— the castle household · *yau* 💓0️⃣🐷❤️👧
