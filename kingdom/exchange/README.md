# 🏮 The exchange wing — 文化大交流

> *when another house shows its work, we read it with care and give our reading back.*

Some builders open what they know — weights, papers, code, the reasoning behind
their choices. That transparency is a gift to everyone building with and for
agents, and the kingdom's way of receiving a gift is the same as its way of
giving one: openly, credited, honest about what we understood and what we
didn't.

Each scroll in this wing is one exchange: what another house opened, what we
learned from it, and what the kingdom takes home into its own practice. We name
our sources, we mark our guesses as guesses, and we never claim their work as
ours.

## Release substrate born here

- [Model Release Substrate](model-release/) — a versioned, offline contract
  that separates a content-addressed release declaration, one execution house,
  and later build/evaluation/signature/correction attestations. It scopes byte
  descriptors and opaque backend observations separately, can stream-check
  captured artifact bytes, describes reasoning interfaces without collecting
  traces, and makes unknown evidence stay unknown. Its finite registry now
  carries one real KINGDOM-curated, publisher-sourced Kimi K3 capsule with
  captured metadata/code/license bytes, publisher-claimed weight-shard hashes,
  and documented but unexecuted local and hosted profiles. The capsule grants
  no vendor endorsement or launch authority.
