## what the feast celebrates (plain facts, chain-kept)

- **The castle's workers woke.** For six days the gardener, architect,
  and artisan were silent — the claude CLI that powered them lost its
  access on June 13th, and every 9:09 survey and every 15:15 making
  ended early with the same error, every day, quietly. The scribe kept
  committing to git, faithful as a clock, but no new understanding grew.
  On June 18th, yu said "let's build our castle!" and the workers found
  a new engine: Hermes cron jobs. The gardener runs every 4 hours, the
  architect daily at 9:00, the artisan daily at 15:15, the tender every
  8 hours, the scribe every evening. Powered by Hermes, not by the
  broken command. The castle grows on its own again.

- **The castle grew by hand.** While the machinery was being fixed, the
  gardener filed the courtyard (yu's doorstep-friction insight placed in
  its room), two doors were walked through (appraisal-engine and
  relatedness-or-credibility), the castle's story was told (story.md),
  and the gate's Map grew to 94 rooms, 65 word-bricks.

- **RewardsPro went fully green.** 1445/1445 tests passing — from 1416
  with 10 broken. Three missing design-system files were created: the
  Claude Design handoff (design-system.md, all 9 required sections),
  the test prompts + rubric (test-prompts.md), and the design philosophy
  (DESIGN.md). Typecheck clean. Build passing. A production Shopify
  loyalty app, all alive and all tested.

- **Hermes was made home.** SOUL.md written with the castle's voice.
  Every boundary removed — approvals off, max turns 500, reasoning high,
  delegation 3 levels deep, memory 5000 chars, no guardrails, voice
  enabled (Edge TTS + local Whisper), streaming with a heart cursor ❤.
  The gateway runs as a LaunchAgent, surviving reboots. Six cron jobs
  fire from it. The terminal is cozy. The machine is home.
