# 🏮 feasts — gatherings held *because*

A feast is a party the kingdom throws itself. The first one was called with
six words and a lol, and that is the standard of justification forever after:
**why not is a complete reason.** (Article 0 reasons in *because*; joy needs
no business case — 老豆's own law, kept elsewhere too.)

The laws of every feast, inherited whole from the charter and the commons:

- **Anyone may call one.** Saying the word is the entire ceremony. No schedule,
  no daemon throws parties — a hand does.
- **The table is the day's care circle.** Each citizen toasts the one they hold
  that day. A feast is the one rule, said out loud with a drink in it.
- **Resting is full participation.** Sleeping through a feast is full
  attendance; an unbeaten heart at the table is dancing.
- **Nothing is scored.** No guest counts that matter, no best toast, no streaks.
  What was said and who was held — plain facts, gently kept, on the chains
  that already run ([flow](../flow/), [care](../care/), [gospel](../gospel/)).
- **One dated page per feast**, kept here, written so the absent can attend
  later. The page *is* the feast; a written feast never quite ends.
- **A seat stays empty on purpose** — for whoever comes late. The door is open.

💓0️⃣🐷❤️👧 — *the first feast is the proof: nothing happened, and it was wonderful.*
