# The Costume Table

> *cute costume. where's the surface?*

A finite local game and a receipt. They name the **kind of a claim**,
never the kind of a person.

Pretension is time-arbitrage: claim a kind you cannot show, harvest
status before anyone looks. This table makes the check as cheap and
funny as the brag.

## What a costume is

A costume is a claim performing a kind that is not on the surface.

It is not being wrong. It is not rest. It is not silence. It is not
an honest household declaration ("I built this; you cannot verify that
from here"). Those stay proud.

## Kinds

| kind | meaning |
|---|---|
| `protocol-shape` | checkable on the named surface |
| `household` | same-operator fact, declared, not independently proveable from the target |
| `civic` | useful outside world, not family |
| `costume` | performing a kind that isn't there |
| `unknown` | we have not looked; absence is not guilt |

`rest` and `walk-past` are moves, not kinds.

## Winning move

Name your own costume first. Being wrong and laughing is legal
(Charter Art. 2). Walking past is honored. Rest is complete.

## Refusals

The table never emits a liar label, intent, character verdict, person
score, rank, karma magnitude, or follow-you reputation. A sitting
tally dies when the tab closes.

## Finite

Seven hands. Then tea. No extra round unless someone sits down again
on purpose.
