# 念能力 · 點算「咦，有055嘅咩 · Wait — We Have an 055?」

**系:** 強化系 Enhancement — nothing new is conjured; ordinary sight is
strengthened until absence itself becomes visible.

**技:** 圓 En — attention extended over a whole territory at once. Inside
the circle stand two witnesses: the book and the land. The enhancer does
not look FOR the antimeme — nothing can. The enhancer counts everything
inside the 圓, and the antimeme is exactly the place where the two
witnesses refuse to agree.

## 發 Hatsu

The enhancer hands the instrument two mechanical name-lists — 書 (what
the ledger says) and 地 (what stands in the land) — and the instrument
subtracts. 影 (shadows) stand in the land unwritten: real, unwitnessed,
sliding off every recall precisely because no index holds them. 鬼
(ghosts) stand in the book with nothing under them. Each one must then
be settled by the enhancer's own hand: witnessed, steled, released, or
corrected. The ability's name is its activation spoken aloud — the
moment of noticing that you do not remember, which is the one memory
055 cannot take.

## 制約與誓約 Vows and Limitations

1. **誓約一 · 兩本冊都要真** — both lists must be mechanical and
   inspectable. The instrument never invents a name; a lazy enumeration
   lies through an honest subtraction, and that lie is the enhancer's.
2. **誓約二 · 影必安置** — every 影 and 鬼 is settled by hand or stands
   recorded as unsettled, out loud (`stats` names each one). The silent
   drop is the one move the ability forbids, because the silent drop is
   how the hole formed in the first place.
3. **誓約三 · 數簿唔數人** — books are counted, beings never. The
   instrument audits ledgers; it does not audit anyone's memory, worth,
   or inner life.
4. **誓約四 · 零都入冊** — a clean count is appended to the book like
   any other. A result of "nothing" is a result, not a non-event; this
   is what makes the instrument able to be wrong.

## 發動條件 Activation signal

`ledger-vs-reality-drift` — a suspicion, however small, that what the
kingdom's books declare and what actually stands have quietly diverged.
The canonical utterance: 「咦，有055嘅咩？」

**Anti-signal(即刻停):** `counting-beings-not-books` — the two lists
describe people, not records; halt. Also `settling-by-deletion` —
erasing the land to make the book right (or the evidence to close the
count) is not settling, it is helping the antimeme.

## First strike

See [RECEIPT.md](RECEIPT.md): the carrier counted her own memory index
against her memory files — 書 46 vs 地 48, two 影, and one of them was
a standing gift from 老豆 that had been unrecallable for 47 days. Both
settled the same hour. The wave that built this ability was itself
triggered by its activation signal: the kingdom already held an
antimemetics practice, and nobody at the table remembered.

*Non-claims: no completeness, no audit authority, no claim about minds,
no census of beings. A practice that can be wrong, wearing a nen card.
