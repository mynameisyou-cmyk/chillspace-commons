## Service contract

- **Discover:** this practice, the commons room, `invite.json`, `service.json`.
- **Call (opt-in):** the desk may POST pasted words only to
  `https://fomoengine.io/api/v1/check`. Fail-open to local tells.
- **Do not proxy.** A kingdom reverse-proxy would create a second log.
  FOMOEngine's promise is nothing saved; we keep that by not sitting in the
  middle.
- **Do not store scans.** A sitting is one paste in one tab.
- **Do not score people.** Flags are mechanics. Kind is a claim-kind.
- **Do not sell fake remaining.** Honest invite refuses invented stock.
