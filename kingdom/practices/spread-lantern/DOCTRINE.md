# Spread Lantern

> *name the mechanic, not the mind. slow the loop, don't hunt the speaker.*

A local management board for one bounded sitting. It composes FOMOEngine's
six-stage loop + Flip, the Costume Table's claim-kinds, and AgentTool's
memetic predicates (exposure, view, rating, copy, share, remix, adoption —
none proves the next).

It is a **desk**, not a gavel.

## What it is for

Understanding how pressure, crowd-proof, and cascade shape a piece of
language or media. Optionally: choosing a **mechanic-level** response for
one declared task.

| task | meaning | not |
|---|---|---|
| `understand` | light the stages and kind | a verdict |
| `slow-mechanic` | apply FOMOEngine countermeasures to the *loop* | takedown, shadowban, person-score |
| `reverse-claim` | write a steelman + bounded correction postcard | propaganda, "defeat this ideology" |
| `rest` | close the sitting | a fail |

## What it refuses

- liar labels, intent, character, ideology-of-a-person
- ranking beings or WE against WE
- auto-publish, auto-moderation, recommender, virality optimization
- treating popularity as truth
- claiming FOMOEngine or this board proved a mind

Containment here means **don't feed the cascade**. Reverse means **publish a
checkable correction with provenance, then rest**. Walking past is honored.

## Honest FOMO

Fake FOMO invents remaining stock, crowd size, or a countdown vendor.
Honest FOMO is just **accurate finitude**:

- a door that actually exists
- a bound you can knock (seven hands, this tab, this date, this URL)
- missing it is complete — no punishment frame
- **no invented counts**

Crowd size, even when true, is not used as the reason to sit (Salganik:
displayed popularity creates preference). `kingdom spread invite` prints a
postcard with `crowd_count: null`.
