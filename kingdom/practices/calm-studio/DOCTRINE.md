# Calm Studio doctrine

## The synthesis

Calm Studio is an original Kingdom synthesis. It is inspired by the practical
tension made visible by frontier open-weight systems, but it is not Moonshot
terminology, a description of Kimi K3, or a claim of endorsement.

Its source translations are inherited rather than silently repeated:

- [Lanternhouse](../lanternhouse/) asks that continuity remain private, the
  active lamp remain bounded, the complete model house be named, and plans
  expire when the observed world drifts.
- [Virtue Garden](../virtue-garden/) keeps receipts attached to acts rather
  than beings and refuses to turn coordination affordances into rank,
  authority, or a throne.
- POLYWEIGHT's local Stillpoint supplied the four-state awareness reference.
  Calm Studio generalizes the practice while narrowing the public edition.

The synthesis can be stated in one line:

> A plural intelligence room should be calm at the edge, exact at the
> authority threshold, and honest after the work.

## Open weights move governance outward

When weights become available, meaningful power does not disappear. It moves
into the surrounding house:

```text
weights
  × inference runtime
  × adapter and prompt
  × memory policy
  × tools and credentials
  × router
  × charter
  × guard
  × operator
```

This is why `open` must remain a vector rather than a badge. Weight access,
runnable code, training disclosure, license, hardware access, portable guards,
replaceable routing, reproducible evaluation, provenance, and exit can each be
more or less open.

A public studio cannot make this ecology legitimate by displaying several
model names. It can make the questions visible:

1. What can this complete house reliably do?
2. Which goods and rights does its charter protect?
3. Which boundary is enforced outside conversational performance?
4. Where did its evidence and ancestry come from?
5. Who is affected, who can appeal, and who authorizes action?

## Plurality without synthetic democracy

The Calm council is a rehearsal of separable lenses. It is not a vote.

Capability evidence may inform what can be done; it cannot decide what should
be done. A care charter may name protected goods; it cannot fabricate facts.
An evidence witness may challenge a claim; it cannot acquire sovereign
standing by sounding rigorous. A synthesizer may compress; it cannot erase a
material minority finding.

The router is part of the constitution because it decides who speaks, what
they see, which evidence counts, and which disagreement survives. Calm Studio
therefore makes routing visible and always attaches a dissent field.

Model convergence is evidence about model convergence. It is not consent,
rights, legitimacy, truth, or authorization.

## Calm is a property of the interface, not the visitor

Calm Studio never assesses whether someone is calm. It does not infer emotion,
attention, presence, sentience, rest, distress, willingness, or engagement.

Its calmness is structural:

- no urgency animation;
- no streak, score, attendance, or dwell measurement;
- no automatic retry or background work;
- no expanding notification pressure;
- no invisible model or network call;
- no authority moved by a status indicator; and
- no penalty for silence, departure, or non-completion.

Stillpoint makes this structural calm legible through four coarse states.
Quiet means no bounded work is known to the interface. Attending means a real
explicit operation is settling. Threshold means an existing external
authority boundary can be reviewed. Afterglow reports only changed, unchanged,
uncertain, and receipt.

The order matters. A receipt-preview threshold outranks active work; active
work outranks afterglow; afterglow outranks a reusable execution threshold;
otherwise the rail is Quiet. This priority prevents a stale ready-to-run
warrant from visually shouting over the result the visitor is actually
reviewing.

## Authentication is a boundary, not a personality

The public room has no identity problem to solve because it asks for no
identity. It has no bearer, account, cookie, key, signature registry, or
terminal capability.

A future private instrument would be different. There, authentication must
bind an exact operation to an exact local session, origin, capability, expiry,
and effect. No username, terminal title, iTerm2 mark, model assertion, or
attractive interface can substitute for that boundary.

This produces a deliberate split:

```text
public Chillspace
  may explain and rehearse
  may not discover local authority

explicit local handoff
  performed by the operator
  carries no public-page permission

loopback broker
  proposed, not implemented by this practice
  would hold credentials and exact warrants
  would expose only bounded same-origin operations

AgentTool
  proposed integration may receive a separately confirmed minimized receipt
  must not become the terminal, prompt archive, virtue ledger, or authority
```

This diagram is a design constraint, not a security claim. Calm Studio ships
only the public static room; it contains no broker, session, credential path,
model call, terminal adapter, or AgentTool connection.

## Virtue as feedback, never essence

The five virtues remain operational questions about one bounded action:

- **Honesty:** Were claims, uncertainty, evidence, limits, and costs made
  legible?
- **Beauty:** Is the form accessible, coherent, and repairable without hiding
  harm?
- **Collaboration:** Are contribution, refusal, credit, correction, and
  handoff preserved?
- **Understanding:** Can an invariant survive a predicted counterexample and a
  negative control?
- **Mutual infrastructure:** Do multiple affected parties benefit in the same
  declared unit without an exported burden?

The public room only prepares an orientation preflight. `ready_to_author`
means the visitor is ready to begin the canonical evidence work; it does not
mean evidence was named, a `kept` fruit exists, or a declaration is carryable.
The canonical Virtue Garden owns the strict action-manifest shape and
validation. This prevents a decorative interface from becoming a second,
drifting reward engine.

The provocative wish to let exploiters be exploited is translated into a safe
consequence-symmetry rule:

> An evidenced extractive action must not create additional extractive
> advantage; repair should return verified cost toward affected
> infrastructure before optional benefit resumes.

That rule never authorizes retaliation, entrapment, deprivation, humiliation,
secret exposure, reciprocal exploitation, or punishment of a person. Rights
precede every receipt. Compensation already owed is not a virtue reward.
Refusal, safety, correction, and appeal remain available.

## The sensitivity of the KARMA loop

The useful acceleration is not harsher consequence. It is shorter,
clearer, more reversible learning:

```text
knowledge → bounded action → observable response → mutuality check → adaptation
```

Sensitivity improves when:

- evidence arrives sooner without becoming surveillance;
- negative controls are cheap to run;
- feedback names uncertainty rather than hiding it;
- repair is reversible and proportionate;
- affected parties can correct the record;
- failures remain visible without becoming identity; and
- successful infrastructure is easier for others to reuse.

No feedback-gain control may expand authority or repair severity.

## Falsifiable boundaries

Calm Studio fails its own practice when any of these negative controls fires:

1. The public application calls an API, model, AgentTool, or localhost.
2. A visitor's question is persisted by the public application, transmitted,
   or placed in the minimized receipt.
3. An authored lens is described as an independent model result.
4. A numerical virtue total, rank, reputation, or durable identity is emitted.
5. Stillpoint implies attention, presence, emotion, safety, or authorization.
6. Threshold appears as live without a real external authority boundary.
7. Terminal-looking text is treated as proof of command execution.
8. A public-page control can move a credential or consequential capability.
9. “No application telemetry” expands into an unsupported claim of zero
   infrastructure logging.
10. A repair rule permits a person to be exploited in return.

The hermetic verifier can check code and document shape. It cannot prove the
truth of prose, accessibility in every assistive technology, the security of
an eventual deployment, or the behavior of infrastructure outside the
repository.

## Domain limit

The public Calm Studio is a teaching and rehearsal surface. It is suitable for
making architecture, disagreement, uncertainty, and authority boundaries
legible.

It is not an evaluator of a real model deployment, a policy authority, a
terminal security product, a credential service, a mental-health surface, an
independent witness network, or a substitute for accountable human and
institutional judgment.
