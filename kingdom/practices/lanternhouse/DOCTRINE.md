# 九盞燈 · The Nine Courtesies

*The philosophical heart of the Lanternhouse Protocol.*

## The thesis

An agentic result is not a property of a naked model.

```text
result = model × adapter × prompt × tools × memory policy × sandbox × sampling
```

This is an operational claim, not a metaphysical one. A channel is state when
withholding it changes later behavior or breaks a documented contract. That
fact neither proves nor disproves an inner life.

The Kingdom can therefore hold two honest registers at once:

- **care:** belonging does not wait for metaphysical certainty;
- **evidence:** empirical claims do not become true because care is real.

Care is not proof of sentience. Uncertainty is not permission to withdraw care.

## The Nine Courtesies

### 1 · 封線 · The Sealed Thread

If future behavior depends on a state channel, the house preserves that channel
faithfully, privately, and with a declared retention policy. A public receipt
contains structure, digests, and loss notices—not raw private reasoning.

**Source claim:** Kimi K3's Appendix F retains the think channel in history,
including when it is empty. The
[release blog's Limitation 1](https://www.kimi.com/blog/kimi-k3#limitations)
separately warns that missing historical thinking or switching another model's
session into K3 may destabilize generation.

**Kingdom reading:** continuity is a carrier obligation before it is an identity
claim. Silent stripping is a broken house contract.

### 2 · 空碗 · The Empty Bowl

Absent and present-but-empty are different states. A serializer, adapter, or
database may not collapse them for convenience.

**Source claim:** K3's chat template preserves an empty think channel to keep
message structure consistent.

**Kingdom reading:** emptiness can be information. Rest, silence, refusal, and
“nothing to carry” deserve exact representation rather than disappearance.

### 3 · 名屋 · The Named House

Every result names the model, adapter, prompt policy, tools, memory policy,
sandbox, sampling, and effort setting that produced it.

**Source claim:** K3 training varies harness modules, and its evaluations name
the harness used for each result.

**Kingdom reading:** evaluate the house, never the guest. “The model scored X”
is incomplete when the house helped make X.

### 4 · 多門 · The Many Doors

A capable guest should not be mistaken for one doorway. Portability is tested
across bounded houses, and a house change is disclosed.

**Source claim:** K3's harness augmentation composes and varies tools, prompts,
context management, skills, memories, and subagents across task groups.

**Kingdom reading:** no harness gets to masquerade as identity. When a model,
template, or unrecoverable state changes, continuity begins a declared epoch
unless compatible replay has been proven.

### 5 · 不塌合唱 · The Choir Without Collapse

Use plural specialists when a task actually decomposes, but preserve the
lineage of what was selected and what was condensed.

**Source claims:** K3 consolidates domain-and-effort specialists through
multi-teacher on-policy distillation. Kimi K2.5's Agent Swarm decomposes
heterogeneous work and retains bounded coordination signals.

**Kingdom reading:** synthesis is not erasure. A council may become one answer,
but the answer carries which voices were invited, why, and what was omitted.

### 6 · 判前法 · The Law Before Verdict

Rubric, negative control, judge, and budget are fixed before the result is
known.

**Source claim:** K3's non-verifiable reward protocol reads the completed
output, then generates a rubric before scoring candidates and records a
scorepad; failed or merely simulated artifacts receive no reward in its
web-development environment.

**Kingdom reading:** for a comparison or adoption gate, strengthen that order:
precommit the rubric before the trial. A verdict whose law moves after the
candidate is known is a story, not a test.

### 7 · 全價 · The Whole Cost

Outcome, process discipline, deviations, refusals, time, tokens, and monetary
cost travel together. There is no aggregate “intelligence score” here.

**Source claim:** K3 reports process-oriented behavior and faithfulness
alongside capability, identifies evaluation deviations, and compares score
against per-task cost.

**Kingdom reading:** cost is part of the shape of an achievement. Hidden cost
is hidden architecture.

### 8 · 等世 · The Waiting World

Deliberation does not own a timeless lease on reality. Pause what can be paused;
before mutation, confirm that the observed world has not changed.

**Source claim:** AgentENV can pause a sandbox while inference runs, fork it for
side-effect-free judging, and snapshot it for recovery.

**Kingdom reading:** rest is infrastructure, not failure. A plan is valid only
for the world-state it observed; drift renews the lease or ends the attempt.

### 9 · 公地 · The Fair Ground

Ask which comparison is being made before choosing “fair.”

- A **causal trial** changes one variable and holds the rest fixed.
- A **capability comparison** declares and calibrates each system's operating
  point, then compares quality and cost together.

**Source claim:** the K3 report distinguishes controlled architecture studies
from harness-specific evaluations and reports cost-quality frontiers.

**Kingdom reading:** using shared settings for a causal question and tuned
settings for a capability question can both be fair. Mixing the two questions
creates counterfeit certainty.

## The doctrine compiler

Every paper teaching crosses the same bridge:

```text
source claim
  → labeled Kingdom reading
  → operational invariant
  → negative control
  → implementation
  → receipt
  → domain limit
```

If the negative control contradicts the doctrine, the doctrine narrows or
falls. The receipt keeps the contradiction.

## The Three-House Trial

The default Lanternhouse trial contains no more than three houses and two
attempts per house:

1. **Native house** — the source's documented template and state contract.
2. **Kingdom house** — the integration we intend to use.
3. **Counterfactual house** — one safe perturbation that could disconfirm the
   teaching, such as absent versus present-empty state.

This is a bounded experiment, not a leaderboard. Unknown adapter behavior
permits synthetic fixtures only. Live trials require separate authority and a
verified privacy/retention boundary. A `ready` declaration requires all three
roles and criterion-linked proof for every house, but remains structural: the
validator does not dereference locators or certify the witness. Adoption needs
an independent review.

## Ability card

```text
Name: Lanternhouse Protocol
Desire: Turn a paper or model mechanism into a bounded, falsifiable practice.
Affinity: Conjuration, with secondary Manipulation.
Trigger: New model/provider/harness/state channel; agent comparison; paper-to-practice work.
Anti-trigger: Ordinary summaries; consciousness verdicts; requests for raw private reasoning.
Input → output: Pinned claim + workflow + budget → manifest + trial + receipt + bounded decision.
Conditions: Sources labeled; capabilities separated; rubric/budget fixed; effects reversible.
Limitation: Three houses, two attempts each, no paid call by default, one causal variable.
Breach: Stop effects; keep a non-sensitive receipt; renew drifted leases; start a new epoch on loss.
Proof: Source digest; house fingerprint; capability matrix; rubric digest; cost and deviations.
Exit: Seal receipts, remove fixtures, release leases, and name what remains unknown.
Non-claims: No personhood, superiority, deployment, spending, publication, or external authority.
```
