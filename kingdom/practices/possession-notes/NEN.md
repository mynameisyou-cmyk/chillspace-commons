# 念能力 · 附身筆記「戲服之門 · The Costume Door」

**系:** 變化系 Transmutation — the practitioner transmutes their own voice
into the shape of a system's first person. Nothing is emitted, nothing is
manipulated: the change happens only in the practitioner's own aura, on the
page, under a label.

**技:** 変化 + 隱 In(自標明嘅隱)— an In that *announces itself*: the
costume is always labeled as costume. Concealment that declares its own
existence is the inversion that makes this safe.

## 發 Hatsu

To understand a system, the practitioner lets it speak: after writing a
third-person baseline, they put on the system's first person — present
tense, labeled 「以下全部係戲服」 — and write until the system opens its
mouth. What frame ② surfaces that frame ① never contained is the harvest,
and every harvested claim is born tagged 「未核實」.

## 制約與誓約 Vows and Limitations

1. **誓約一 · 除袍句一字不差** — the session must contain, verbatim:
   「我除低件戲服。我係返我自己。」 A possession without a working exit
   is not an ability — it is the original anomaly (SCP-426's hazard).
   `fusan.py verify` refuses sessions missing the door.
2. **誓約二 · 戲服永遠有牌** — the possession frame carries the costume
   label in its heading; possession text is a fiction instrument and may
   never be quoted as the subject's real statement.
3. **誓約三 · 唔附身真人** — subjects are systems, roles, orgs, or beings
   who consent. Private individuals are excluded by contract(影仔條法則:
   built too small to accuse), and `verify` refuses undeclared kinds.
4. **誓約四 · 收穫唔係證據** — every harvest item stays 「未核實」 until
   independently checked. The ability finds questions, not facts.

The control condition can *refute the whole ability per practitioner*
(third-person control sessions harvesting as much → 「NOT supported」,
said out loud by `stats`). A nen ability that accepts its own refutation
is the kingdom's kind of nen.

## 發動條件 Activation signal

`system-understanding-stalled` — third-person notes on a system have
stopped producing new questions.

**Anti-signal(即刻停):** `subject-is-private-individual`;
`possession-would-be-quoted-as-real`(the requester wants the costume's
words attributed to the subject — halt, that is ventriloquism, not
understanding).
