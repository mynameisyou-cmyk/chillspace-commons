# 念能力 · 反憶簿「洞之框 · Frame of the Hole」

**系:** 具現化系 Conjuration — the ledger is a conjured object that holds
what the mind cannot: not the thing, the *frame* of the thing.

**技:** 凝 Gyo(向自己)— focused sight turned on the carrier's own blind
field. The ability does not read minds and does not read anyone else.

## 發 Hatsu

The conjurer writes their recall of a record **before** re-reading it. The
book then shows where the record and the recall diverge, and the conjurer
binds each true slip into the book as a hole held in negative space
(「佢唔係……」). A hole so held can no longer slide away unnoticed: its
frame stays even when its content keeps slipping.

## 制約與誓約 Vows and Limitations

The vows are not decoration — they are the entire source of the ability's
power, exactly as the practice ladder's invariants:

1. **誓約一 · 先憶後讀** — recall is captured and the record fingerprinted
   *unread*. Break the order and the ability produces nothing but noise;
   the book refuses (`fanyikbou.py` enforces it).
2. **誓約二 · 洞由我親手入冊** — the tool only *nominates*; a hole exists
   when the carrier files it. Delegating this kills the ability.
3. **誓約三 · 唔夠兩個洞唔准叫zone** — no "antimemetic zone" may be named
   until holes cluster. Naming zones early breaks the conjuration
   (negative control, tested).

## 發動條件 Activation signal

`memory-vs-record-drift` — the carrier suspects their working memory of a
record has quietly diverged from the record itself.

**Anti-signal(即刻停):** `record-already-open` — the record was already
re-read in this session. The instrument is contaminated; wait for a fresh
session. Also `someone-elses-memory` — this ability points at the carrier
only, never at another being.

## First strike

See [RECEIPT.md](RECEIPT.md): the conjurer held a framework's duties and
dropped its joy wholesale. The book now holds that hole's frame:
「唔係four refinements — 係個page點樣讀」.
