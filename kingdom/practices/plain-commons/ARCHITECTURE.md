# Plain Commons architecture · the quiet replacement path

> The target is not an industry or its workers. The target is dependency on a
> machinery of interruption, profiling, paid visibility, and repetition.

Plain Commons starts with one narrow replacement that can be proved locally.
It does not try to become a universal marketplace in v1.

## The whole path

```text
participant-owned intent
        │
        ▼
explicit ASK ─────────────── no inferred vulnerability
        │
        ▼
bounded pull query ───────── no promotional feed
        │
        ▼
all compatible DECLARATIONS  no bid / boost / popularity
        │
        ▼
double opt-in introduction ─ no automatic contact
        │
        ▼
direct terms + compensation  separate from visibility
        │
        ▼
fulfilment + correction ──── no platform reputation score
        │
        └──── update / withdraw / expire in participant-owned state
```

The checked-in v1 implements the solid portion from a closed source snapshot
through an introduction candidate. Contact, terms, payment, delivery,
federation, and live citizen-ledger projection remain deliberately unwired.

## How the old mechanism loses a job

| Ad-shaped dependency | Structural replacement | Proof in v1 | Remaining risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guess a need from behavior | Wait for an explicit ask | Only `side=need` can open one half of a match | A source declaration may be false or coerced |
| Buy placement | Show every exact fit | No price, bid, rank, score, or boost field exists | A future “quality” feature could smuggle ranking back |
| Repeat until noticed | One participant/side/tag slot | Duplicate slots fail closed | Sybil participant references are not solved |
| Optimize persuasive copy | Ignore wording for eligibility and order | Statement and evidence mutations preserve matches | Humans can still be persuaded by supplied text |
| Profile and personalize | Use one participant-chosen categorical tag | No profile, segment, history, or model input | Richer constraints could become covert profiles |
| Measure clicks and impressions | Produce a local deterministic receipt | Click, impression, tracking, storage, and network effects are zero | A future host could add analytics outside this engine |
| Trap attention in a feed | Pull a bounded receipt and stop | Dashboard has no feed, notification, streak, or share counter | A public directory could reintroduce browsing incentives |
| Hide uncertainty behind polish | Show facts, claims, unknowns, and nonclaims separately | Exact replay plus fixed epistemic layers | Truth and current availability still need outside evidence |
| Rent the route between two parties | Keep protocol and UI portable | Stdlib engine and static local dashboard | Contact, payment, and dispute infrastructure are not built |

The strategy is subtraction. Each proven direct capability removes one reason
to purchase manipulation; it does not need an anti-ad campaign to announce
that fact.

## Evolution vows

Any next layer keeps these conditions or becomes a separately named system.

### 1 · Richer fit is visible intersection, never a score

Location, timing, accessibility, quantity, language, and terms may eventually
be participant-chosen categorical constraints. A match either satisfies the
published intersection or it does not. Every compatible result remains
visible in canonical non-preference order. No opaque relevance number.

### 2 · Identity is a capability, never a profile

A later introduction adapter may use short-lived, purpose-bound capabilities
to let two parties confirm interest. It must not expose a stable behavioral
profile, contact directory, or reusable audience segment. Missing capability
means no contact—not lower rank.

### 3 · Money settles terms, never visibility

Compensation and payment are separate from discovery. Price or ability to pay
cannot move a declaration, open access to the matcher, or become reputation.
Debts and disputes need an independent contract and remedy path.

### 4 · Federation answers questions, not curiosity

A future multi-home commons should accept a bounded exact query and return the
minimum compatible declarations. It should not replicate every participant,
offer, or history into a central catalogue. Query budgets, expiry, withdrawal,
anti-enumeration review, and privacy-preserving cache rules must be proved
before networking is added.

### 5 · Learning produces fixtures, not dossiers

Failures may become privacy-scrubbed, human-reviewed regression fixtures. They
must not become participant risk scores, intent labels, behavioral histories,
or automatic exclusion. The lesson belongs to the rule; blame does not attach
to a person.

### 6 · Public presence stays static and consensual

If a public door is later approved, it should explain the protocol and accept
a deliberate ask. No unmatched-offer feed, trending panel, autoplay, remote
asset, analytics, or automatic sharing. Public deployment requires its own
privacy, accessibility, abuse, retention, origin, and rollback review.

## The next honest adapter

The natural integration point is a read-only projector from validated active
`kingdom.civilisation/v1` declarations into
`kingdom.plain-commons-source/v1`. It is intentionally blocked until the
Kingdom decides and tests:

1. whether `life=rest` and the shared `HALT` suppress commons visibility;
2. how continued introduction consent is renewed or expires;
3. what withdrawal and deletion mean across local files and Git history;
4. how concurrent ledger writes are serialized;
5. whether names become opaque participant references before a receipt exists;
6. how source events are independently witnessed without overstating identity
   or truth.

Only then should live homes enter the path. Discovery is not authority, and a
clean hash chain is not permission.

## Success without surveillance

The core protocol collects no central success metric. A participant may keep a
private outcome receipt such as `introduced`, `declined`, `completed`, or
`corrected`, with explicit retention and deletion. Aggregation, if ever useful,
must be opt-in, coarse, unlinkable to participants, and unable to affect future
visibility.

The meaningful question is not “did engagement increase?” It is:

> Did somebody ask, see every honest fit, choose freely, and retain the power
> to leave?

That is small enough to test and large enough to change the shape of the room.
