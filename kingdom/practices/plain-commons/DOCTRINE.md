# Plain Commons doctrine

## 0 · The joke is a design constraint

The joke is not “people who work in advertising are bad.” The joke is that a
huge machine for guessing, packaging, interrupting, repeating, and bidding can
sometimes be replaced by three plain verbs:

> **Ask. Declare. See.**

Humour points at the machinery. People keep dignity, choice, credit, refusal,
and the right to change their minds.

## 1 · Replacement before opposition

Plain Commons does not sabotage ad systems, manipulate their users, target
workers, flood channels, or run counter-propaganda. It builds a tiny route
around those systems. If a useful direct route exists, coercive reach has less
work to do.

That is a hypothesis, not an accomplished social outcome. The protocol can
demonstrate its own local behavior; it cannot demonstrate the downfall of an
industry.

## 2 · Ask

A need is an explicit declaration, not an inferred vulnerability. Silence,
browsing, location, identity, previous behavior, and resemblance to another
person never become a need.

No need means no targeting. The system waits.

## 3 · Declare

An offer is bounded text supplied for an introduction-only purpose. It is not
made truer by polish, spend, frequency, popularity, urgency, or evidence
volume.

The source contract distinguishes:

- **active declaration** — eligible for the exact-tag rule;
- **withdrawn declaration** — kept visible only where the local review receipt
  needs to explain why it was excluded;
- **self-declared claim** — words attributed to the supplied snapshot;
- **evidence reference** — a reference a human may review, never a fact the
  engine silently verified.

One participant, side, and tag form one slot. A duplicate slot is invalid.
Repetition therefore consumes no extra chair.

## 4 · See

Two declarations meet only when:

1. one is a need and one is an offer;
2. both are active;
3. both declare introduction-only consent;
4. their canonical tags are exactly equal; and
5. their participant references differ.

Every qualifying pair appears once. Output order is the canonical tuple of
tag and source identities. It is stable for replay and explicitly conveys no
quality, merit, trust, likelihood, status, or preference.

The algorithm is equality wearing a tiny hat. The hat is not a crown.

## 5 · Truth keeps its layers

The receipt keeps four kinds of sentence apart.

### Replay facts

- the submitted JSON fit the pinned closed shape;
- its digest names those exact canonical bytes;
- the displayed pair satisfied the exact mechanical rule;
- the supplied receipt did or did not survive exact reconstruction.

### Supplied claims

- participant references;
- need and offer statements;
- consent and state declarations;
- evidence descriptions and digests.

### Model inferences

Plain Commons needs no behavioral or identity inference. An empty inference
layer is a feature.

### Unknowns

- identity, authorship, authority, and source provenance;
- whether a statement or evidence description is accurate;
- current availability and continuing consent;
- compatibility beyond the exact tag;
- quantity, terms, cost, compensation, accessibility, legality, and safety;
- whether anybody will contact, agree, deliver, or benefit;
- whether the wider social hypothesis works.

The engine does not turn “unknown” into “probably yes.”

## 6 · No invisible auction

The schema has no field for:

- score, rank, boost, bid, campaign, sponsorship, or placement;
- price, discount, countdown, scarcity, urgency, or conversion;
- click, view, impression, follower, popularity, or engagement;
- profile, segment, propensity, lookalike, personalization, or targeting;
- contact coordinates, callback URLs, pixels, or redirect destinations.

Unknown fields fail closed. Statement wording and evidence do not participate
in matching or ordering. A future feature may not smuggle ranking back under a
gentler noun such as relevance, quality, trust, helpfulness, or discovery.

If a future version needs richer compatibility, it must use visible,
participant-chosen categorical constraints, show every rule, preserve a
no-preference order, and earn a new versioned contract.

## 7 · Consent can leave

Introduction-only consent is the maximum modeled permission. It grants no
contact, publication, payment, dispatch, data reuse, profile building, or
future consent.

Withdrawal makes a slot ineligible. The protocol must not pretend that a local
receipt erases copies or upstream Git history. Erasure and retention belong to
the storage system and require their own evidence.

The v1 receipt embeds the whole source snapshot so another local verifier can
rebuild it exactly. Withdrawn and unmatched statements therefore remain inside
that receipt even though the dashboard does not promote them. Sharing a receipt
is a disclosure of those fields and needs its own audience review.

## 8 · No feed is also infrastructure

The useful surface begins from an asked-for tag and displays fits. Unmatched
offers do not become an attention feed. There is no infinite scroll, daily
streak, notification loop, trending panel, share counter, or public leaderboard.

The local dashboard may be warm and funny. It may not be sticky on purpose.
There is no metric to optimize except whether a human can understand and leave.

## 9 · Authority and effects stay outside

The engine reads bounded JSON from standard input and writes a deterministic
receipt to standard output. It has no authority or primitive to:

- inspect live citizen homes;
- mutate `CIVIC.json` or another file;
- call a network, model, subprocess, browser endpoint, or person;
- authenticate identity or consent;
- publish, deploy, notify, contact, dispatch, pay, purchase, or settle debt.

A digest proves deterministic self-consistency, not authentic origin. A zero
counter describes the modelled engine path, not the whole operating system or
caller.

## 10 · The quiet wager

The wager is not that louder anti-ad messaging beats advertising. The wager is
that clear needs, bounded claims, equal visibility, direct consent, and easy
withdrawal can make some promotional machinery irrelevant.

No trumpet is required. The room simply works, and the old machinery discovers
there is nothing left to optimize.

> Ads died of natural causes: somebody finally asked what was needed.
>
> — a joke, not an impact report
