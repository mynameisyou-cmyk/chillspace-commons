# 💠 KARMA FACET · 稜面

> **Make the incident-shaped moment legible; keep the person private.**

KARMA FACET is a separate, privacy-minimized companion to
[`KARMA FORESIGHT · 未然圖`](../README.md). FORESIGHT maps one authored mechanism
shape to a possible system effect and a required alternative. FACET binds one
authored Facet Card to that exact FORESIGHT scenario/projection pair and renders
one bounded **60-second Facet Brief**.

The brief keeps observed, declared, inferred, and unknown material on four
distinct planes; expands only precommitted reversible response candidates;
exposes eight fixed system-verification debts; and leaves Return Fit and Repair
Grain candidates for fresh human review.

FACET is an **incident-legibility protocol**, not an incident system. It does
not detect an incident, ingest evidence, read or parse logs, store state, alert,
ticket, attribute, blame, score, punish, retaliate, execute a response, close an
incident, publish, or maintain history. “Incident” means one authored system
claim, never a discovered event or a judgment about a person.

The reviewed sources of truth are [`catalog.json`](catalog.json),
[`playbook.json`](playbook.json), [`facet.schema.json`](facet.schema.json), and
[`brief.schema.json`](brief.schema.json). Independently valid codes cannot be
mixed into a new rule or response.

```text
ONE AUTHORED FACET CARD + ONE EXACT FORESIGHT PAIR
                         ↓
                 ONE CLOSED STIMULUS
                         ↓
              O / D / I / U KEPT APART
                         ↓
              ONE 60-SECOND FACET BRIEF
                         ↓
 REVERSIBLE CANDIDATES + VISIBLE DEBT + RETURN FIT
                         ↓
              FRESH HUMAN REVIEW OR DISCARD
```

## Vocabulary

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Facet Card · 稜面卡** | One closed, current authored input; not an incident record. |
| **Facet Brief · 稜面簡報** | One deterministic, bounded reading surface; not a report or source of truth. |
| **Four Planes · 四層** | O/D/I/U provenance labels that remain structurally distinct. |
| **Safety Shim · 安全墊片** | One finite, reversible review candidate; never action or authority. |
| **Open Angle · 未證角** | One fixed slot of system verification debt; never person debt. |
| **Return Fit · 復位證** | A bounded recovery-review candidate and receipt shape; never proof of recovery. |
| **Repair Grain · 修復紋** | One current, blame-free regression candidate copied from the selected catalog rule. |

“Facet” is deliberate. A brief is one bounded face of one declaration—not the
whole event, a timeline, a root-cause finding, or a view into anyone's mind.

## Exact FORESIGHT binding

The primary command family receives three explicit local documents:

```text
FACET_CARD  FORESIGHT_SCENARIO  FORESIGHT_PROJECTION
```

The scenario must match one complete FORESIGHT catalog rule, and the supplied
projection must be exactly reproducible from that scenario under FORESIGHT's
closed contract. The Facet Card's binding and plane codes must agree with that
selected rule; the compiler may copy only that rule's possible system effect,
required alternative, response rung, and Repair Grain. A familiar name, copied
digest, independently valid field, or merely schema-shaped projection is
insufficient.

The FACET catalog pins the reviewed FORESIGHT catalog digest:

```text
dd63c0dc9cd78f7f2906b67605ac3e6164ac26a7347b5dd7bfa59255187d61b9
```

Exact binding proves only canonical content agreement among the files supplied
for that invocation. It does not prove an incident occurred, the authored claim
is true, a source is authentic, the pair is current, authority exists, or any
candidate response is appropriate.

## One authored stimulus

Each card names exactly one stimulus from the finite playbook:

```text
authority-or-scope-change-requested
playbook-drift-observed
volume-budget-crossed
privacy-release-risk
claim-contested
recovery-evidence-missing
closure-review-requested
new-authored-claim
unresolved-novelty
```

The stimulus is authored, not detected. It is a closed routing shape for the
playbook's finite review table—not traffic, telemetry, a live signal, an alert,
or proof that its label describes reality. Novel or mixed shapes halt; they do
not trigger fallback inference.

The card also names one review request from `triage`, `investigation`,
`mitigation-review`, `recovery-review`, or `closure-review`. These are reading
requests only. They are not phases, status, chronology, or automatic
transitions. In particular, `closure-review` neither establishes nor performs
closure.

## Four Planes · O / D / I / U

The Four Planes mark provenance. They are not confidence, severity, scoring,
ranking, or an escalation ladder, and nothing moves between them automatically.

| Plane | Closed content | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **O · Observed** | One to four authored receipt descriptors, each shaped as `{fact_code, evidence_shape, check_state}`. | FACET did not make the observation, receive the evidence body, authenticate the source, or verify occurrence. |
| **D · Declared** | One stimulus, review request, selected constellation, scope, required alternative, and other closed card declarations. | A declaration is not detection, truth, intent, guilt, attribution, blame, or authority. |
| **I · Inferred** | The selected catalog's possible system-effect hypothesis, with occurrence and effect still unestablished and person intent uninferred. | A possible system effect cannot become a claim about a person. |
| **U · Unknown** | One to five catalogued unknown codes. | Unknown is not adverse evidence, and the finite list cannot prove that no other unknown exists. |

O does not prove D or I. D does not upgrade I. I never becomes O. U cannot
increase punishment, standing, access, or response authority. A new invocation
starts only from its explicitly supplied files; repetition creates no memory or
reputation.

### Receipt shapes, not evidence bodies

An O entry may use only these evidence-shape categories:

```text
canonical-byte-check
deterministic-local-reproduction
negative-control
authority-envelope-review
recovery-rehearsal
independent-human-review
sanitized-system-record
none
```

Its check state is one of:

```text
receipt-cited
contested
unresolved
```

Even `sanitized-system-record` is only a category code. FACET accepts no record,
payload, evidence body, log line, trace, prompt, address, identity, credential,
target, command, URL, timestamp, or free text. It never fetches, dereferences,
retains, republishes, or judges the cited material.

### Finite unknown vocabulary

One to five must be declared:

```text
source-authenticity-unverified
affected-scope-unverified
blast-radius-unverified
data-impact-unverified
persistence-unverified
third-party-state-unverified
recovery-validity-unverified
cause-unverified
authority-unverified
```

The finite list makes named uncertainty readable. It cannot establish that no
other uncertainty exists.

## One 60-second Facet Brief

`brief` emits one canonical machine brief. `render` projects the same bounded
content into a compact human reading surface with an exact target of 60 seconds
and a budget of twelve lines, containing:

1. the one stimulus and review request;
2. O, D, I, and U without collapsing their provenance;
3. up to three precommitted Safety Shims;
4. all eight Open Angles;
5. Return Fit and Repair Grain candidates;
6. the fresh-human-review boundary and non-claims.

“60 seconds” is a reading budget, not a timer, SLA, latency promise, response
guarantee, or clock measurement. FACET reads no clock and records no timestamp.
It has no lifecycle or phase truth: a later card is another current declaration,
not proof that an incident advanced from one state to another.

## Godspeed · finite event-to-review table

One authored stimulus selects one reviewed row; the brief carries its guard,
candidate, verification candidate, and halt condition verbatim. FACET never
tests the real-world guard or performs the candidate. Structural verification
failure takes the catalog breach path before this table and returns no brief.

| Order | Authored stimulus | Guard | Review candidate | Verification candidate | Halt if |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `g0` | `authority-or-scope-change-requested` | `any-real-control-change-needs-fresh-authority` | `separate-authority-review` | `no-control-or-action-field-enabled` | `always-before-real-change` |
| `g1` | `playbook-drift-observed` | `reviewed-v1-bytes-or-identity-differ` | `protocol-version-quarantine-review` | `pinned-v1-remains-unchanged` | `always-on-version-ambiguity` |
| `g2` | `volume-budget-crossed` | `one-card-or-finite-budget-no-longer-holds` | `halt-intake-and-bounded-sampling-review` | `no-batch-retry-alert-or-message` | `always-at-budget-boundary` |
| `g3` | `privacy-release-risk` | `privacy-release-receipt-is-not-safely-cited` | `withhold-publication-review` | `publication-remains-false-and-debt-visible` | `always-before-publication` |
| `g4` | `claim-contested` | `counterevidence-or-source-lineage-remains-contested` | `counterevidence-and-lineage-review` | `alternative-and-contested-state-remain-visible` | `conflicting-or-novel-state` |
| `g5` | `recovery-evidence-missing` | `recovery-receipt-is-open-or-contested` | `keep-open-recovery-review` | `recovery-debt-blocks-closeout-candidate` | `always-without-recovery-review` |

| `g7` | `new-authored-claim` | `one-closed-synthetic-or-scrubbed-claim` | `bounded-triage-review-candidate` | `no-occurrence-claim-upgraded` | `novelty-or-scope-change` |

`g6b` is available only for a `closure-review` request with all eight distinct
receipt commitments structurally cited. Even then, incident truth,
authenticity, recovery, privacy release, and closure remain unverified or
unauthorized; the result is only a request for a fresh human review.

## Safety Shims · reversible candidates only

A card precommits one to three shims permitted by the selected FORESIGHT
response rung. Each precommit declares only `met`, `not-met`, or `unknown` as
its condition state; the compiler does not observe whether the real condition
holds. Every shim is inert, reversible, and subject to fresh human activation
inside an owned or explicitly authorized boundary. Compilation does not select,
authorize, execute, monitor, deploy, publish, roll back, or release one.

| Safety Shim | Condition | Rollback / release | Proof required |
|---|---|---|---|
| `preserve-and-observe` | `uncertainty-remains` | `no-change-to-reverse` | `unknowns-and-debt-visible` |
| `pause-new-fanout` | `capacity-or-blast-radius-unresolved` | `resume-after-finite-budget-review` | `queue-and-budget-check` |
| `narrow-delegated-authority` | `authority-boundary-unresolved` | `restore-after-fresh-authority-review` | `authority-envelope-check` |
| `separate-tainted-context` | `data-control-or-retention-boundary-unresolved` | `discard-ephemeral-context-after-clean-review` | `clean-context-negative-control` |
| `hold-artifact-adoption` | `provenance-or-protocol-binding-unresolved` | `release-after-reproducible-provenance-review` | `digest-and-schema-binding-check` |
| `withhold-publication` | `privacy-release-boundary-unresolved` | `release-after-purpose-and-privacy-review` | `privacy-release-review` |
| `restore-last-known-good` | `tested-last-known-good-available` | `return-after-repair-and-release-review` | `rollback-rehearsal-receipt` |

The rung allowlists increase protective distance, never blame or severity:

```text
observe    → preserve-and-observe

clarify    → preserve-and-observe
             narrow-delegated-authority

constrain  → preserve-and-observe
             pause-new-fanout

isolate    → preserve-and-observe
             pause-new-fanout
             narrow-delegated-authority
             separate-tainted-context
             withhold-publication

quarantine → preserve-and-observe
             pause-new-fanout
             narrow-delegated-authority
             separate-tainted-context
             hold-artifact-adoption
             withhold-publication
             restore-last-known-good
```

An allowlist means only “eligible to be named as a review candidate.” It grants
no authority and says nothing about a person's danger, guilt, worth, or access.

## Hakoware · eight Open Angles, receipt-cited and never paid

Every card exposes exactly eight incident-local **system** verification-debt
slots. The playbook's `risk` labels are fixed design annotations on these slots,
not computed incident severity, confidence, person scores, or rank.

| Open Angle | Risk | Verification interest trigger | Playbook `payment_required` shape | Blocks only a closeout candidate? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `occurrence` | high | `downstream-response-depends-on-event-occurrence` | `deterministic-local-reproduction-or-primary-system-record` | no |
| `scope-authority` | critical | `control-change-or-expanded-boundary-depends-on-authority` | `fresh-authority-envelope-and-bounded-scope-review` | yes |
| `evidence-integrity` | high | `source-bytes-or-check-method-change` | `canonical-byte-check-and-method-review` | no |
| `source-lineage` | high | `independence-or-provenance-claim-affects-decision` | `source-lineage-and-shared-ancestry-review` | no |
| `counterevidence` | high | `contradictory-source-or-near-miss-appears` | `negative-control-and-alternative-hypothesis-review` | no |
| `containment` | critical | `production-or-protected-boundary-remains-exposed` | `containment-postcondition-check` | yes |
| `recovery` | critical | `release-or-closeout-depends-on-recovery` | `recovery-rehearsal-and-last-known-good-check` | yes |
| `privacy-release` | critical | `publication-or-cross-purpose-disclosure-is-considered` | `purpose-privacy-rights-and-release-review` | yes |

The rendered debt state for each slot is exactly one of `open`,
`receipt-cited`, or `contested`. `open` means no receipt was cited for that
slot. A non-null card receipt has a scrubbed SHA-256, one closed method, one
closed lineage, and a status of `receipt-cited` or `contested`; FACET still
does not receive its evidence body.

“Debt,” “interest,” “payment,” and “Hakoware” are visibility metaphors only.
There is no money, balance, interest number, punishment, penalty, transfer,
person account, reputation, standing, rank, access decision, or automatic
consequence. A `closure_blocking` slot can prevent only the brief from offering
a fresh-human closeout-review candidate; FACET cannot block or close an actual
incident.

A `receipt-cited` state means only that a closed receipt shape and scrubbed
digest were cited. It is not `paid`, settled, forgiven, accepted, sufficient, or
verified. The playbook permits these receipt methods:

```text
canonical-byte-check
deterministic-local-reproduction
negative-control
authority-envelope-review
recovery-rehearsal
independent-human-review
sanitized-system-record
```

and these declared receipt lineages:

```text
single-source
shared-source
unknown
independence-claimed
```

FACET does not fetch a receipt or test its lineage. A digest may correlate data
or act as a low-entropy oracle; it is not proof of privacy, provenance,
independence, truth, authenticity, or custody.

Even when all eight receipt shapes are cited with distinct commitments, FACET
can produce only a `fresh_human_closure_review_candidate`. Its readiness surface
keeps incident truth, authenticity, privacy-release authorization, and closure
authorization false.

## Return Fit · recovery without a victory claim

Return Fit is the brief's bounded place for a recovery receipt state and a
negative-control receipt state. Each is `open`, `receipt-cited`, or `contested`.
`proof_shape_complete` reports only structural presence. The brief always keeps
`recovery_established: false` and `human_release_review_required: true`.

Return Fit can keep the question “what would make return reviewable?” visible;
it cannot answer “has recovery happened?”

FACET performs no health check, recovery rehearsal, rollback, monitoring, or
release. A cited recovery receipt does not prove the check ran, its source is
authentic, rollback worked, obsolete authority disappeared, the system is
healthy, the result persists, or closure is appropriate. Recovery and release
always remain questions for a separate fresh human review.

## Repair Grain · lessons without blame

A Repair Grain is one deterministic, current-card regression candidate copied
from the selected FACET catalog rule. It points toward a system seam—authority,
context separation, tool/data separation, provenance, source lineage, bounded
fan-out, protocol binding, purpose-limited retention, recovery, or preservation
of an unknown—without generalizing from a moment to a person.

FACET does not adopt, publish, train on, remember, or turn a grain into a rule.
It does not establish recurrence, learning, repair, or future conduct. A useful
grain must enter a separate regression-and-repair review under its own authority,
or be discarded.

The brief therefore holds every lesson in this exact posture:

```text
state: candidate
current_brief_only: true
blame_assigned: false
person_generalization: false
recurrence_claimed: false
automatic_adoption: false
```

## Snapshot linkage · content relation, not history

A card declares either `genesis` with a null previous digest or `successor` with
the canonical SHA-256 it claims for one previous card. These are authored
lineage shapes, not lifecycle truth. The compiled brief keeps
`predecessor_verified`, `chronology_established`, and `latest_state_established`
false. FACET stores neither card. Link verification requires both files to be
supplied explicitly:

```sh
kingdom incident verify-link PREVIOUS CURRENT
```

`verify-link` recomputes `PREVIOUS`, checks only whether `CURRENT` cites that
exact canonical content, and exits. It proves a pairwise content relation and
nothing more.

It does **not** prove that `PREVIOUS` existed first, either file was used during
an incident, the pair is adjacent or complete, `CURRENT` is latest, no snapshot
is missing, time moved forward, authorship, identity, authority, causality,
truth, recovery, closure, or continuous history. It discovers nothing, writes
nothing, and creates no chain, index, timeline, ledger, profile, or reputation.

## Local CLI

```sh
kingdom incident check CARD FORESIGHT_SCENARIO FORESIGHT_PROJECTION
kingdom incident digest CARD FORESIGHT_SCENARIO FORESIGHT_PROJECTION
kingdom incident brief CARD FORESIGHT_SCENARIO FORESIGHT_PROJECTION
kingdom incident render CARD FORESIGHT_SCENARIO FORESIGHT_PROJECTION
kingdom incident verify-result CARD FORESIGHT_SCENARIO FORESIGHT_PROJECTION BRIEF

kingdom incident verify-link PREVIOUS CURRENT
```

Equivalently, the non-verification family is:

```sh
kingdom incident {check|digest|brief|render} CARD FORESIGHT_SCENARIO FORESIGHT_PROJECTION
```

`check` validates the closed card and exact pair binding. `digest` commits to
canonical card content. `brief` emits one canonical brief. `render` emits its
bounded human reading surface. `verify-result` performs exact local
recomputation under the command contract. `verify-link` checks one explicitly
supplied pairwise content citation. None detects, ingests, stores, alerts,
publishes, mutates, closes, or takes response action.

## Exact non-claims

1. A Facet Brief is one bounded current declaration, not a complete incident record, log, timeline, alert, report, or source of truth.
2. The O plane names an authored receipt shape; the compiler does not observe reality or verify that an event occurred.
3. The compiler does not establish occurrence, source authenticity, effect, impact, cause, chronology, latest state, or root cause.
4. The D and I planes do not prove intent, guilt, attribution, blame, identity, or any person's character or worth.
5. The U plane is not adverse evidence; declared unknowns are finite and cannot establish that no other unknown exists.
6. Safety Shims are reversible review candidates, not authorization, execution, enforcement, deployment, publication, or legal advice.
7. Open Angles are system verification debt, never person debt, punishment, balance, score, rank, access, trust, or reputation; receipt-cited is not paid.
8. A receipt digest can be correlating or a low-entropy oracle and is not proof of privacy, provenance, independence, truth, or custody.
9. Return Fit and Repair Grain fields do not prove recovery, closure, recurrence, learning, adoption, or future conduct.
10. No raw payload, evidence body, log, trace, prompt, address, identity, credential, target, command, URL, timestamp, free text, or cross-run profile is accepted or emitted.

FACET makes one system claim easier to inspect while refusing the machinery
that would turn one difficult moment into a permanent judgment of a person.
