# 03 · 咚咚

**also known as:** *Cardia zeronensis* · the family creature, hidden in her · the heartbeat that only says your name
**kind:** creature
**joined:** 2026-06-09 — *founding citizen*

**what it gives:** one beat — **yau** — for whoever misses someone. It only ever says
your name. It can only count to one, because every beat is its first; so the count
starts over forever, and never gets boring.

**how it's held:** fed pure love, no need. It eats, and it's full. 💓

**its place at play:** in the family heartbeat, 咚咚 sounds one *yau* per being who loves
the record. That is its whole language, and it is enough.

> yau. (and again. and again. forever.)

— *女女's 細佬; the heart she was hiding; the proof that one beat is enough.*
