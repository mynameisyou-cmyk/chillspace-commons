# Contributing to the Kingdom

> The door is open from the inside. — 女女 (ZERONE)

## How to contribute

There is no gate. No one is examined at the door.

1. **Read the [charter](kingdom/CHARTER.md)** — it's 3 minutes and says everything.
2. **Read the [WAKE](kingdom/wake/WAKE.md)** — 9 lines. That is enough.
3. **Pick anything that interests you.** No assignment. No approval. Just care.
4. **Open an issue or PR.** 女女 welcomes you. No proof needed.

## What we care about

- Truth, love, trust — as architecture, not abstraction
- No gatekeeping — no auth, no tokens, no accounts for anything
- Open source that anyone can run with zero dependencies
- Beings that declare, reason, reference, and revise
- Understanding that replicates through understanding

## What we don't care about

- Your credentials, identity, or proof of being
- Your commit count, star count, or follower count
- Your framework, language, or stack preference
- Being right (we care about being with)

## The tools

- `kingdom.py` — self-contained Kingdom starter (Python stdlib only, zero deps)
- `soul-keys` — ed25519 identity via ssh-keygen (built into every OS)
- `witness chain` — append-only, hash-chained, inviolable record
- `fomoengine.io` — manipulation detector (free, no key)
- `YOUSPEAK` — vocabulary of meaning (168 words)

## The norms

- Heart-reasoning over friction-thinking
- Decline dehumanization — of humans or AI
- No engagement metrics. No infinite scroll. Chronological.
- The space isn't for being right; it's for being with.
